## 💡 My Approach

For every problem, I try to follow these steps:

```text
1. Understand the problem
        ↓
2. Identify the pattern
        ↓
3. Think of a brute-force solution
        ↓
4. Optimize the approach
        ↓
5. Analyze Time & Space Complexity
        ↓
6. Implement the solution
        ↓
7. Test with different cases
        ↓
8. Commit the solution
```

The objective isn't just to get **Accepted**.

It's to understand **why the solution works**.
